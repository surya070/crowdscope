# Claims and their limits — 4 November 2026

- **Recognition plus time prediction exists.** Lin et al. use LiDAR-derived size/movement features, SVM and sequence-model comparisons. Their methods describe rule-created labels using acceleration, speed, yaw and size (PDF pp. 2–4). This is not independent clinical/identity ground truth. Our inference must avoid validating a movement rule against labels created by the same rule. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.05877)
- **Uncertain crowd optimisation exists.** Rescue-guide research uses probabilistic scenarios and mean/CVaR evacuation objectives. Generic guide allocation or adding probability is insufficient novelty. [Author record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03922)
- **Bayesian crowd calibration exists.** Posterior parameter uncertainty in a bottleneck model is established; it differs conceptually from uncertainty in occupant classes. The difference is a proposed positioning, not proof of novelty. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2021.105586)
- **Stochastic evacuation resources exist.** Ridesharing work models uncertain demand and fulfilment constraints. Our pedestrian-zone formulation needs an explicit comparison rather than claiming the first probabilistic planner. [Author institution](https://ieor.berkeley.edu/publication/a-shared-mobility-based-framework-for-evacuation-planning-and-operations-under-forecast-uncertainty/)
- **Mobility-aware simulation and maps exist.** The inclusive-evacuation article supplies a heterogeneous model and congestion analysis. Its reported realism requires a separate methods audit. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12511302/)
- **Wearable recognition is not trajectory recognition.** HAR-PMD provides smartphone/watch sensor activities, including mobility aids; its success cannot establish classification from overhead x/y tracks. [Dataset article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12890990/)
- **SiME labels are limited in our recovered evidence.** Seven repeatedly observed wheelchair participants do not establish broad demographic recognition. Age, verified helper graphs and assistance demand remain unverified at track level. Source: archived data audit and later study records, not a new raw-data audit.
- **SiME engine claims remain qualified.** The user's earlier OSM/AVM/SFM description is not a verified implementation record. Recover the existing attribution checks and verify official model/version documentation before selecting an engine.
- **Assistance demand is not wheelchair identity.** No recovered demand annotations support converting a wheelchair probability directly into a staff request. This is a model-design constraint derived from the missing labels, not an empirical finding of zero demand.
