# Stage 2 decision

Date: 2 November 2026. **Conditional go for empirical development analysis; no-go for a definitive empirical relationship comparison or a large simulator rebuild yet.**

We have recoverable measured trajectories, metadata geometry and some wheelchair identities. Six saved trials have matching metadata counts and all tracked downstream crossings: 1301, 1501, 1503, 1505, 1507 and 501. This is a coverage finding, not proof of independent cohorts or adequate sample size. Only one of those six has the seven verified wheelchair mappings used in this audit. A two-wheelchair-trial forecast evaluation is further constrained by the incomplete record in 507.

Next development work (Stage 3): reproduce measured speed/spacing/headway patterns, quantify start/end coverage, and investigate the 507 missing crossing and 1307 count mismatch using source documentation. Expand the label registry only through verified documentation. Inspect archive metadata for candidate free-movement or additional wheelchair trials without inspecting new outcomes before assigning their role. No claim that those trials are untouched or participant-independent yet.

Need before a protocol freeze:

- Reconcile cohort membership and incomplete trajectories.
- Establish whether more suitable wheelchair trials/free-speed records exist and can be acquired legally.
- Decide observation boundary and target; prevent future roster and arrivals from leaking into features.
- Choose a grouped evaluation unit acknowledging repeat participants. Seven labelled wheelchair people cannot support strong claims about broad unseen populations by themselves.
- Complete the outstanding closest-paper checks from Stage 1.

Assistance/family analysis: currently unavailable as an empirical test. No confirmed helper graph or family labels. Do not invent one healthy helper per wheelchair user. Qualitative descriptions alone do not supply per-trial operation states.

Age-based analysis: currently unavailable at individual-track level. Published demographics support motivation but cannot train or validate per-track age features without a verified mapping.

Wheelchair recognition: at most a small feasibility pilot after grouping and label completeness are settled. Track-height or recording artifacts could make apparent accuracy misleading; a trajectory-only experiment must exclude such shortcuts and evaluate unseen people if possible.

The prior synthetic negative findings remain preserved. This audit neither establishes a new positive finding nor validates our former rigid compound model.
