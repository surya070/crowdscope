# Prospective compound-movement feasibility design

29 October 2026. User authorised testing movement before a revised prediction protocol. This is a software/engineering test design, **not** a frozen prediction protocol. No new predictor, held-out scenario or study outcome may be generated in this stage. Preserve all previous failed recovery artifacts.

## Representation selected before tests

Implement an original, prescribed-route, time-stepped rigid-footprint kinematic model using existing Shapely. This refines the earlier fine-grid candidate: continuous polygon footprints avoid introducing a spatial occupancy grid solely for these limiting tests. Do not call it a reproduction of a published CA model or JuPedSim. [Sarmady et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.00082), full methods read, describe multi-cell directional body maps and heterogeneous speeds, including assisted wheelchairs. That motivates explicit footprint representation; their density-speed calibration and their results are not reproduced or adopted here. No source code was copied or new package licence accepted. Existing dependency notices remain intact.

Each individual has a circular footprint. An attached wheelchair/helper unit occupies the convex hull of their two circular footprints, with fixed 0.75 m centre separation. Wheelchair/client radius is 0.36 m; helper radius is 0.24 m. Both occupants remain separately identified and counted. Synthetic speeds: supported pair 1.0 m/s, helper ceiling 1.1 m/s; individual test speeds are scenario-specific and explicitly recorded. No demographic norms or physical pushing validation are claimed. These are engineering fixtures, not study parameter estimates.

The pair pivots about the client's centre, at at most 90 degrees/s and at most helper_speed/0.75 radians/s. During a pivot the client is stationary. Translation and rotation are separate actions; neither receives unused movement credit after waiting. Individual circles may change heading without footprint rotation. Shared-pace family fixtures remain separate collision units, with the parent's commanded pace capped to the child's; the queue test checks their spacing only in that prescribed single-lane situation.

Prescribed waypoint routes isolate movement/footprint mechanics from autonomous path finding. They are a material limitation. Each accepted translation reserves the convex swept footprint. Rotation is subdivided into at most two-degree arcs; each arc's endpoint hull is inflated by the maximum-vertex-radius sagitta, conservatively covering intermediate motion. Circular body polygons are circumscribed rather than inscribed. All complete footprints and sweeps must stay inside geometry and avoid every other unit's current footprint. Sequential deterministic front-first reservations prevent simultaneous conflicting moves; results are conditional on that scheduler, not an empirical crowd law. No positions are projected or teleported.

An individual's exit is recorded when its complete circle clears the measurement plane. A compound unit persists until both members clear. An already counted exited member may remain in the unit's computational outflow footprint until its helper clears; it is not counted twice. Keep active-occupant plus exited-occupant conservation at every update. Provide enough outflow space beyond the fixed measurement plane; never move the exit to rescue a case.

## Cases and preselected checks

Test one pedestrian; one unassisted wheelchair; one attached pair in free space; the pair through a 1.0 m straight doorway; a 90-degree turn in a 2.4 m corridor; a mixed single-lane queue with an adult, older-profile occupant, parent/child fixture and assisted pair; and a negative control with a 0.60 m doorway narrower than the 0.72 m client diameter. The inaccessible negative control must retain both occupants through the 40 s test cap, with no artificial exit.

Use time steps 0.04, 0.02 and 0.01 s. All positive cases must clear. Adjacent-step clearance differences must be <=0.5 s, a preselected engineering tolerance unrelated to publication or safety standards. Free straight-case individual/pair last-clearance times must match the independently calculated distance/speed result within two integration steps. Positive turn time must match translation plus separately limited quarter-turn time within five integration steps. All initial and accepted footprints must be inside geometry, without material inter-unit overlap (area tolerance 1e-10 m² for floating-point boundary noise). Both pair-member centres must remain 0.75 m apart within 1e-9 m. Member travel between updates must respect the prescribed individual/support ceiling. Family centre gap in the single-lane fixture must remain <=1.10 m. Check every update, not sampled frames alone.

Also repeat all cases at the finest time step with circle polygon resolution doubled from 16 to 32 segments per quadrant. Clearance changes must be <=0.5 s, physical checks must still pass and the negative control must still be blocked. These resolution tests are bounded engineering evidence, not a proof of global convergence or empirical accuracy. Add adversarial swept-rotation/translation collision tests and occupant accounting tests. All failed tests remain reportable; no scenario/limit may be silently revised to pass.

If the gate passes, pause for stage review before freezing a new prediction protocol. The new protocol must specify route information, scheduler assumptions, family behaviour, scenario distributions, fitting/test partitions, censoring and external-validity limits before final prediction outcomes. This feasibility pass alone is insufficient to claim a novel publishable paper.
