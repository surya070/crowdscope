Evidence and boundaries

This replay uses synthetic JuPedSim trajectories from a development case. It is not a validated evacuation forecast. Its clearance remains timestep-sensitive. Actual SiME data separately support 42 observed occupancy checkpoints; human trajectories are excluded from this demo.

CCTV input, automatic wheelchair/child recognition, dynamic dispatch and evacuation timing require additional models and validation. No operational staffing or safety recommendation is made here.

CrowdScope · RV University Research Methodology project · All probabilities and service requests in this demonstration are scenario assumptions.
